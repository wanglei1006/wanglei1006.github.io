Research interests

Selected themes

My work spans visual understanding and human-centered modeling, with applications in animation, scene perception, medical imaging, and sensing.

3D humans & digital animation

Human mesh recovery, pose and shape estimation, speech-driven facial animation, and motion retargeting.

Visual & scene understanding

Point cloud segmentation, visual relationships, 3D object detection, open-vocabulary perception, and SLAM.

Medical imaging & intelligent sensing

Medical image segmentation, dental image analysis, biometrics, action recognition, and flexible pressure sensing.

Efficient learning & image processing

Neural network quantization, graph neural networks, image enhancement, and lossy-to-lossless image compression.
